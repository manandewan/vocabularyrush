# Vocabrush — The Ultimate CAT VARC Root & Tone Decryption Platform

**Vocabrush** is a high-yield, systematic vocabulary and tone decryption platform custom-engineered for **CAT VARC** (Common Admission Test - Verbal Ability & Reading Comprehension).

---

## 🌟 Key Architecture & Features

### 1. 6x6 Systematic Root Engine (36 Interactive Lessons)
* **6 High-Yield Thematic Categories**:
  1. *Tone, Sentiment & Author Attitude* (`BENE/MAL`, `PLAC/PUGN`, `LUC/OB`, `LAUD/CULP`, `GRAV/LEV`, `FID/TREP`)
  2. *Discourse, Rhetoric & Argumentation* (`DICT/VOC`, `LOQU/GARR`, `VER/FALL`, `HER/GRESS`, `QUER/MON`, `NEG/PROV`)
  3. *Mind, Cognition & Epistemology* (`GNO/SCI`, `PATH/SENT`, `MEM/OBLIV`, `CRED/DOG`, `VOL/COG`, `SPEC/INTUIT`)
  4. *Power, Governance & Social Dynamics* (`CRAC/ARCH`, `EQU/JUR`, `CULP/SOLV`, `SED/STA`, `DOMIN/SERV`, `CIV/DEMO`)
  5. *Time, Evolution & Universal Scale* (`CHRON/TEMP`, `MORPH/MUT`, `PAN/PAUCI`, `GEN/MORT`, `FLU/CED`, `MAGN/BREV`)
  6. *Epistemic Disciplines: -OLOGY & -ITICS* (`EPISTEMOLOGY/ONTOLOGY`, `TELEOLOGY/ESCHATOLOGY`, `AXIOLOGY/TAUTOLOGY`, `HERMENEUTICS/EXEGESIS`, `SEMIOTICS/DIALECTICS`, `HEURISTICS/POLEMICS`)
* **300+ Root Lexemes & Derivatives** with prefix/root/suffix breakdowns, definitions, authentic CAT sentences, and audio pronunciation.
* **6 Section-Wise Mastery Exams** testing holistic comprehension with percentage scoring.

### 2. High-Yield Supplementary Tracks
* **CAT Tone Taxonomy & Decryption Suite**: Calibrates the severity spectrum across critical tones (*Vitriolic vs Acerbic vs Sardonic vs Disparaging vs Trenchant*).
* **High-Frequency Latin & Academic Signposts**: Demystifies logical operators (*A Priori vs A Posteriori*, *Status Quo*, *Ad Hominem*, *Ipso Facto*, *Sui Generis*, *Caveat*, *Prima Facie*).
* **CAT Secondary Meaning Trap Vault**: Defuses traps on polysemic words where academic writing differs from colloquial speech (*Qualify, Pedestrian, Plastic, Arrest, Sanction, Checked, Currency, Flag*).

### 3. Multi-Panel Lesson Runner ("Next" $\rightarrow$ "Next" $\rightarrow$ "Next")
Each lesson is structured into 6 sequential panels:
1. *Concept & Strategy Hook*
2. *Root Family A Deep Dive*
3. *Root Family B Deep Dive*
4. *Morphological Contrast & Trap Matrix*
5. *Authentic CAT Reading Comprehension Context*
6. *Dedicated Checkpoint Quiz Panel* (with 3-tier Socratic Clues)

### 4. Local Browser Persistence & Backup
* Progress across all 39 lessons and exams is saved locally in `localStorage`.
* **Export Backup**: Download your study streak as a `.json` backup file.
* **Restore**: Import your progress into any browser or mobile device seamlessly.
